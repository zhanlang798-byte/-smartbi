(08/04/2026). The energy price index eased 1.1% in July, weighed down by declines in coal (-4.8%) and crude oil (-2.2%), partly offset by a 19.1% surge in European natural gas prices. The non-energy index remained broadly stable (+0.3%). Food prices changed little (-0.3%), fertilizers declined 4.3%, beverage prices rose 17.4%, while raw materials slipped 0.5%. Metals fell 2.8%, and precious metals retreated 5.2%.
