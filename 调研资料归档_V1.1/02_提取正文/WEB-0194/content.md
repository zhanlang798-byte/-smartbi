[CPI for all items increases 0.1% in July; shelter rises](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm "CPI for all items increases 0.1% in July; shelter rises")

08/12/2026

In July, the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers rose 0.1 percent, seasonally adjusted (SA), and rose 3.4 percent over the last 12 months, not seasonally adjusted (NSA). The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in July (SA); up 2.5 percent over the year (NSA).
